# Azura Tools v0.10.48

## What's new since v0.10.44

- **Farm History:** New launcher tab with searchable, saved farming sections. Each entry shows the character and class, map, duration, gold and EXP totals, and average rates per minute and hour. Recording has its own switch and does not depend on whether the Gold/EXP widgets are visible.
- **Gold Dropped and Exp Acquired:** The widgets now show gains per minute and per hour. Their totals and rates freeze for up to 30 seconds after the local character dies or the map/channel changes. Selecting another character clears the cards; dealing damage starts a new section.
- **Build Export:** Fixed unrestricted inspections that arrived as an unresolved player RPC after a map transition. Saved builds remain separate from the temporary **YOU** card.
- **Boss DPS:** Encounter duration now displays whole minutes and seconds after one minute, consistently across overlay and history.

## Install or update

1. Download `AzuraTools-v0.10.48-win-x64.zip` below and extract the **entire** archive.
2. Run `azura-tools/AzuraTools.exe` from the extracted folder.
3. Install [Npcap](https://npcap.com/#download) for live capture. [AutoHotkey v1.1 64-bit](https://www.autohotkey.com/download/1.1/) is needed only for Hotkey Macro.

Settings, saved builds, encounter history, and farm history stay in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\AzuraTools`; they are not included in the ZIP. Node.js and Bun are not required.

## Validation

- All 161 automated tests passed. The 554 files in the local application folder and release ZIP match by SHA-256. No development documentation or personal data is included.
- Live capture confirmed that the current game still sends gold and EXP balances. The new freeze and Farm History flow still needs a full in-game test.
- A reported intermittent absence of the temporary **YOU** card was not reproduced. The selection packet, compiled capture worker, and local build service all accepted the character; this behavior remains under investigation.
